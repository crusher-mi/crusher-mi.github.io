Learning Style

I learn by observing, listening and doing.

My preferred learning approach combines observation, active listening and practical application. I understand concepts better when I can see how something works, listen to an explanation, and then apply it myself.

01

Observing

I prefer to observe how a concept, technology or solution works before applying it myself. Seeing an example helps me understand the structure and process more clearly.

02

Listening

Listening to explanations and discussions helps me understand ideas from different perspectives and identify the important details of a new concept.

03

Doing

Practical work is an important part of my learning. After understanding a concept, I prefer to apply it through coding, projects and hands-on practice.

My Learning Process

My learning process usually moves from understanding to practice. I first observe and listen, then I apply what I have learned by doing something practical.

01 Observe
02 Listen
03 Understand
04 Do
05 Improve

Applying It to Technology

When learning a new programming language, framework or technical concept, I prefer to first see an example, listen to an explanation, and then implement the idea myself. Practical application helps me turn what I have learned into experience.
